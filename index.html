Three questions, and why these three

Question one

What do people come to you for?

Not what you are good at on paper. What people actually knock on your door about. That is your real skill, and it is usually invisible to you because it costs you no effort.

Question two

What angers you about this world?

Anger is the most reliable compass anyone has. The thing that makes you furious at 2 a.m. is the thing you will still care about in fifteen years, when the prestige has worn off.

Question three

What makes time stop?

The task where you look up and three hours have gone. Flow is not a mood, it is data — it tells you which method you belong in, regardless of which subject you love.

Skill, anger, flow. Where the three overlap is not a personality quiz result — it is a shortlist of real programmes with real deadlines, filtered by what you can actually afford, where you can actually live, and what you are actually eligible for today.

The year, laid out

Deadline calendar

Most opportunities are missed for one reason: the application window opened while you were revising for an exam and closed before you looked up. Deadlines shift year to year — always confirm on the official page before you plan around a date.

Where your answers are kept

There is no account, no server and no database. This is a set of static files. Nothing you type is ever transmitted anywhere — there is nowhere for it to go.

Two things are stored, both on this device only:

  • Your answers and shortlist live in this browser's local storage, so the page remembers you between visits. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • Your answers are also encoded into the page address once you finish the questions. That is what makes a plan bookmarkable — and it is also how you move it between devices: send yourself that link and open it on your phone, and your results come back exactly as they were. It is the closest thing to an account, without one.

Because there is no account, a shortlist saved on your laptop will not appear on your phone by itself — copy the link across, or use Copy my plan as text on the results page. Nothing here uses cookies, analytics or trackers of any kind.
