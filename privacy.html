Privacy

The short version: there is no account, no server, no database and no analytics. Nothing you type here is transmitted anywhere, because there is nowhere for it to go.

What this site is

A set of static files — HTML, CSS, JavaScript and fonts — served by GitHub Pages. All the ranking, filtering and writing you see happens inside your own browser. No request carrying your answers is ever made, because no such request exists in the code.

What is stored, and where

On your device only

  • Your answers and your shortlist are kept in this browser's local storage so the page remembers you between visits. Clearing your browser data removes them, and nothing anywhere else changes when you do.
  • Your theme choice and whether you have seen the tour are kept the same way.
  • An offline copy of the site is saved by the service worker if you install the app or simply revisit it. That copy is the site's own files. It never contains your answers.

In the page address

Once you finish the questions, your answers are encoded into the URL. That is what makes a plan bookmarkable, and it is how you move one between devices: send yourself the link, open it on your phone, and your results come back as they were. It is the closest thing to an account without having one.

It follows that anyone you send that link to can see those answers. They are in the link itself. Send it to yourself freely; think twice before posting it somewhere public, the same way you would with any other link that carries your own words.

What is not here

  • No cookies. None are set, so there is nothing to consent to.
  • No analytics, no tracking pixels, no third-party scripts. Nobody counts your visit, here or anywhere else.
  • No advertising, and nothing is sold. There is no data to sell.
  • No fonts, images or scripts loaded from another domain. Everything is served from this one, so no other company sees that you were here.

What your host can see

GitHub serves these files, so GitHub's own server logs will record that a request was made, the way they do for every site they host. That is outside this site's control and is described in GitHub's privacy statement. It records the request for a file, never anything you typed — the answers in your URL are the fragment after #, and browsers do not send that part to the server at all.

Links to other sites

Every programme card links to that programme's own official page. Once you follow one of those links you are on someone else's site, under their rules, and nothing on this page applies there any more.

Children

Nothing is collected from anyone, of any age, so there is nothing here to treat differently.

Changes

If this ever stops being true — if analytics or any third-party service is ever added — this page will say so before it happens, not after. As of the date below, none of it is here.
