Terms of use

This is a free, independent index. You can use it for anything you like. In return it asks one thing: check the official page before you plan around anything you read here.

The one that actually matters

Every deadline, amount and eligibility line here is a starting point, not the authority. Programmes move their dates, change their rules and occasionally close without notice. This index is re-checked in passes, and the date of the last pass is shown on the front page and on the deadlines view, deliberately rendered from the data rather than typed into the page, so it cannot quietly disagree with itself.

A wrong date is worse than no date, because someone who believes a call closed in January will not look again in May. So: the official page linked on every card is the authority, and this one never is. If the two disagree, the official page is right and this site is out of date. Telling us is welcome; acting on the official page is what protects you.

Rankings are opinions, and are labelled as such

The tier badges and the written verdicts are judgements about how much a programme would change for a given person. They are held in a separate file from the facts, on purpose, so that anyone can disagree with a grading without disturbing the underlying data. Reasonable people will disagree. Nothing here is a prediction that you will or will not be admitted.

This is not advice

Nothing here is legal, immigration, financial or medical advice, and nothing here is a substitute for your college's own guidance or a programme's admissions office. Visa rules, licensing routes and loan terms in particular are individual, change frequently, and are worth confirming with someone whose job it is.

Your data, and what you may do with the site
